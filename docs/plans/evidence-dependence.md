# Evidence dependence: lineages, not observation counts

Status: accepted direction (2026-10-23; OpenSpec change `model-evidence-dependence`)
Related: [provenance kernel plan](provenance-kernel.md), [temporal semantics](temporal-semantics.md),
GitHub issue #9, Bead `data-processing-4ry`.

Observation count is not independent-corroboration count. A claim repeated along a mirror
chain (pixiv → danbooru → gelbooru → archives) or byte-identical files served by five
providers are many *observations* of one *assertion lineage*. This document records the
decisions that keep the two counts distinguishable, so future confidence or projection
logic cannot over-count copied claims by default.

## Decisions

**D1 — Dependence derives from the relationship graph; rows carry no lineage stamps.**
A dependence between two sources is itself a claim: observed (a provider declares its
source), verified (byte equality), derived, inferred (a matcher proposes a repost), or
reviewed. That is exactly what a Phase D relationship row is — typed, epistemic,
evidence-backed, review-gated, append-only. A lineage/group identifier stamped on evidence
or observation rows would be immutable-but-derived (membership changes as edges arrive),
a parallel mechanism beside the relationship ledger, and uncertainty-free (an id cannot say
how well established the dependence is). Evidence rows stay single-finding facts; the
sha256 inside a `same_bytes` evidence row is detector payload, not lineage identity.
Lineage is recomputed at read time and is always consistent with the current retained graph.

**D2 — The vocabulary classifies into descent, equivalence, and structural.**

| Class | Kinds | Lineage effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Descent | `reposted_from`, `derived_from`, `variant_of`, `reencoded_from`, `resized_from`, `cropped_from`, `censored_variant_of`, `edited_from`, `transcoded_from`, `extracted_from`, `thumbnail_of` | merges (the subject's content and its claims about that content likely descend from the target's) |
| Equivalence | `same_bytes`, `same_work` | merges (common origin proven, direction unknown) |
| Structural | `page_of`, `parent_of` | never merges (a quote or reply is new authorship, not a copy of the parent's claims) |

Structural *anchoring* (distinct from structural kinds) always applies: a post, its media
occurrences, and its work anchor are one node for lineage purposes.

**D3 — The default dependence policy is conclusion-grade and non-rejected.** An edge counts
as dependence when its kind is in the default set (descent ∪ equivalence), its epistemic
status is `observed`, `verified`, `derived`, or `reviewed`, and its review state is not
`rejected`. Deterministic seeds land `verified`/`observed` with review state `pending`
(they bypass *required* review, they are not auto-confirmed), so filtering on
`confirmed` would wrongly exclude every seeded edge. An `inferred` edge is a pending
proposal and must not drive a conclusion; confirming it promotes the status to `reviewed`
and merges. A `rejected` edge has been overturned; the sides stand independent again while
the edge and its evidence remain retained. Every parameter is an argument — a surface that
wants different dependence states its own policy (see `lineage_components` in
`src/media_catalog/relationships/dependence.py`).

**D4 — Corroboration counting is a projection concern.** `lineage_components` returns
groups, never confidence. A future corroboration surface states its dependence policy in
its projection spec (participating in the spec digest like every stated policy) and counts
distinct lineages intersecting its claim anchors. "N sources agree" is never assembled
implicitly; treating observations as independent must be an explicit stated choice.

**D5 — No global provider-reliability scores.** Dependence is claim- and field-specific (a
site may mirror uploads but originate tags). Global scores would encode a wrong
generalization and age badly; per-edge dependence with evidence is the record, and any
reliability heuristic would be a projection over that graph, never a stored ranking.

## Worked example: the mirror chain

Generated by running `lineage_components` over the mirror-chain fixture in
`tests/test_evidence_dependence.py` (not hand-transcribed). Four platforms form the gh#9
chain — danbooru reposts pixiv, gelbooru reposts danbooru, an archive reposts gelbooru —
plus two independent sources; every post observes the same claim.

```text
CLAIM: favorite_count = 42

Observations (6 retained, all kept):
  pixiv:1001  favorite_count=42  at 2026-01-01T00:00:00Z
  danbooru:2001  favorite_count=42  at 2026-01-01T00:00:00Z
  gelbooru:3001  favorite_count=42  at 2026-01-01T00:00:00Z
  archive:4001  favorite_count=42  at 2026-01-01T00:00:00Z
  pixiv:1002  favorite_count=42  at 2026-01-01T00:00:00Z
  danbooru:2002  favorite_count=42  at 2026-01-01T00:00:00Z

Lineage components under the default dependence policy:
  lineage: ["occurrence:1", "occurrence:2", "occurrence:3", "occurrence:4",
            "post:archive:4001", "post:danbooru:2001", "post:gelbooru:3001", "post:pixiv:1001"]
    edge: reposted_from  post:archive:4001 -> post:gelbooru:3001
    edge: reposted_from  post:danbooru:2001 -> post:pixiv:1001
    edge: reposted_from  post:gelbooru:3001 -> post:danbooru:2001
  lineage: ["occurrence:5", "post:pixiv:1002"]
  lineage: ["occurrence:6", "post:danbooru:2002"]

Distinct lineages backing the claim: 3  (observation count: 6)
```

Four observations of the chain remain four retained observations and resolve to **one**
lineage; the two independent sources contribute one lineage each: six observations, three
independent corroborations. The byte-identical case is the equivalence class in the same
derivation: five providers whose occurrences resolve to one verified asset merge through
seeded `same_bytes` edges with no declared source URL at all
(`test_same_bytes_equivalence_merges_without_declared_sources`).

## Uncertainty is preserved

The dependence itself carries epistemic status and review state, so a lineage is only as
strong as its weakest accepted edge — and the policy can be tightened per surface:

- an `inferred` repost proposal leaves the sides separate until review confirms it
  (then it merges, with the decision retained in the ledger);
- a `rejected` dependence separates the sides again, while the edge and its evidence stay
  queryable;
- a surface may still state `include_rejected=True` or widen the status set — its choice,
  its responsibility, stated in its projection spec.

## Follow-ons (not in this change)

- As-of lineage: derivation currently reads current relationship state; once relationship
  conclusions gain as-of reconstruction (2ai follow-on), lineages can be pinned under an
  evidence cutoff.
- The first corroboration-counting projection: a new policy slot ("dependence") stating
  the edge set, statuses, and rejected-edge handling; the count then participates in the
  projection digest.
- Whether asset-endpoint relationships need traversal once an asset-level kind exists
  (today the vocabulary links occurrences).
